Meaning: Sentences within a paragraph belong to and support the same idea. Paragraph breaks give the reader’s brains a nanosecond to synthesize that idea and move on. Visual impact: Paragraphs arrange the meaning on the page or screen. The breaks offer welcome white space. Our eyes notice the density of the words. Voice: Paragraph breaks contribute to rhythm and flow. Like a subtle movie soundtrack, the paragraph structure can set a mood—especially when a single sentence paragraph stands alone in a sea of longer ones.
